Built for Huntsville's Specific Rodent Challenges

Huntsville Rodent Control was founded in 2023 by a team with deep roots in North Alabama. After years watching national pest control chains apply one-size-fits-all programs to a city with genuinely unique rodent pressure, humid subtropical climate, mature hardwood canopy, NASA and defense facility neighbors, rapid residential expansion. We decided to build something local.

We know that a Twickenham home built in 1905 faces completely different roof rat risk than a Hampton Cove subdivision finished in 2019. We know that an office building on Cummings Research Park has different compliance requirements than a restaurant near Big Spring Park downtown. And we know that Monte Sano's wooded ridgeline sends a fresh wave of roof rats toward the city every fall as temperatures drop in the Tennessee Valley.

That local knowledge doesn't come from a training manual. It comes from working in Huntsville neighborhoods, learning the canopy patterns, understanding the construction eras, and treating the rodent pressure that's actually here. Not a national average.

How We Operate

Every job starts with a free inspection. We walk the property, identify the species, locate entry points, and tell you what we find before recommending anything. If the problem is straightforward, we'll tell you that. If it requires multi-phase exclusion work, we'll explain exactly why.

We follow Alabama structural pest control regulations for any chemical treatments. We don't fabricate credentials, invent license numbers, or promise outcomes we can't deliver. What we promise is honest assessment, transparent pricing, and service Monday through Saturday, 7AM-10PM.

Our service area is honest too. We cover Huntsville, all of Madison County, and genuinely nearby communities in Limestone, Morgan, Marshall, and Jackson counties, within about 45 minutes of our base. We don't pad the list with Birmingham or Nashville.
