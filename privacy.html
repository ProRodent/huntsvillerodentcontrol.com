Privacy Policy

Huntsville Rodent Control · huntsvillerodentcontrol.com · Last updated: January 2025

This privacy policy describes how Huntsville Rodent Control ("we," "us," or "our") collects and handles information when you visit huntsvillerodentcontrol.com.

Information We Collect

We do not operate contact forms on this website. The primary method of contact is by phone. As a result, we collect very limited information from website visitors:

  • Automatically collected data: When you visit our site, standard web server logs may record your IP address, browser type, referring URL, pages visited, and time of visit. This data is used solely for site performance monitoring.
  • Phone calls: If you call us at (844) 635-0403, we may retain call-related information such as your phone number, call duration, and any notes taken during the call for the purpose of scheduling and delivering service.
  • Analytics: We may use privacy-respecting analytics tools to understand aggregate traffic patterns. These tools do not identify individual visitors.

How We Use Information

We use information collected from this website only to:

  • Understand how visitors use our website and improve its content
  • Schedule and deliver rodent control services to customers who contact us
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies

Our website may set basic cookies to support analytics and site functionality. These cookies do not track you across other websites and do not store personally identifiable information. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect how some pages function.

Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services including:

  • Google Fonts: Loads typefaces from Google's servers. Google's privacy policy applies to these requests.
  • Google Maps Embed: Used on our contact page. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Call tracking: We use call tracking technology to measure the effectiveness of our marketing. This may associate phone numbers with website sessions for analytics purposes.

Data Retention

Server log data is typically retained for 30-90 days. Customer contact information related to service delivery is retained for a reasonable period for business and legal purposes.

Children's Privacy

Our services and website are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your Rights

If you have questions about information we hold about you, or wish to request its deletion, please contact us at contact@huntsvillerodentcontrol.com or by calling (844) 635-0403.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
